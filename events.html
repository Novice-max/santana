Our Vision

To develop students who will act as transforming agents nationally and globally.

Our Mission

To provide an excellent curriculum, wide range of co-curricular activities and a caring diverse learning environment.

At Santa Ana Calm Waters Academy, we strive to build a strong foundation for every child by equipping them with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to succeed in life.

Our motto, "Serving God and Humanity Through Education," reflects our dedication to instilling core Christian values while providing quality education that prepares students for the future.

We aim to create a nurturing environment where students can thrive academically, socially, and spiritually.

Our commitment to discipline and care ensures that each child receives the attention they need to succeed.

Join us in our quest to nurture the leaders of tomorrow and make a positive impact in our community.
