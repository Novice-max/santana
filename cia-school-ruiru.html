About CIA School (Santa Ana Calm Waters Academy)

Welcome to CIA School — officially known as Santa Ana Calm Waters Academy, the premier private educational institution in Ruiru, Kiambu County. Our school is commonly referred to as "CIA" or "Santana School" by the local community and has been serving families since 2017.

Located strategically along Githunguri Road near Ruiru Law Courts in the peaceful Two Rivers Estate, CIA School offers a serene learning environment that promotes both academic excellence and character development.

Why Choose CIA School in Ruiru?

🎓 Full CBE Curriculum

CIA School offers the complete Competency Based Education (CBE) from Reception to Grade 9, aligned with Kenya's national standards.

✝️ Christian Values

Our motto "Serving God and Humanity Through Education" reflects our commitment to developing students with strong moral foundations.

📍 Prime Location

Conveniently located near Ruiru Law Courts on Githunguri Road — easily accessible from Ruiru town, Kahawa, Juja and surrounding areas.

👨‍🎓 Experienced Staff

Qualified teachers led by Director Mr. Anthony Maina and Manager Madam Christine, dedicated to nurturing each student's potential.

🚌 School Transport

Safe and reliable school bus services covering Ruiru, Kimbo, Membley, Thika Road and surrounding areas daily.

🏆 Holistic Development

Sports, cultural activities, computer classes, French lessons, and spiritual development programs beyond academics.

Educational Programs at CIA School Ruiru

👶 Reception (Ages 3–5)

Early Childhood Development with play-based learning for foundational skills.

📚 Grades 1–3 (Foundation Level)

Lower Primary CBE building reading, writing, numeracy and basic life skills.

📖 Grades 4–6 (Intermediate Level)

Upper Primary CBE with class projects and KNEC assessments.

🎯 Grades 7–9 (Junior Secondary)

Junior Secondary CBE with life skills and career guidance to prepare for senior secondary.
