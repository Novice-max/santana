About Santana School — Your Trusted Educational Partner in Ruiru

Welcome to Santana School — officially known as Santa Ana Calm Waters Academy, but lovingly called by many names in our community. Whether you know us as Santana School, CIA School, Calm Waters Academy, or Santa Ana School, we are the same institution dedicated to providing quality CBE education in Ruiru since 2017.

How to Find Santana School

📍 Address: Two Rivers Estate, Githunguri Road, Near Ruiru Law Courts

🚗 Driving: From Ruiru town, take Githunguri Road towards Law Courts — we are just past the courts on your right.

🚌 Matatu: Take any matatu to "Ruiru Law Courts" and ask the conductor to drop you at "Santa Ana" or "CIA School." 2-minute walk from there.

From Nairobi: Thika Highway → Ruiru exit → follow signs to Law Courts → Githunguri Road.
